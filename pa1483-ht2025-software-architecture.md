# Assignment Policy on Plagiarism and GenAI

## No Plagiarism

We expect students to properly cite and credit any material used in your report.
Check out the [Refero project](https://refero.lnu.se/english/) if you are unsure about how to do this.

**Possible Consequences:**

  * We may call for an oral follow-up to discern the extent of intentional or unintentional plagiarism.
    Negative outcomes result in a failing grade and a report to BTH's disciplinary committee.


## Responsible Use of AI

We expect students to use any AI tools responsibly.
The goal of this assignment is not to deliver an _artifact_ (the report),
but to have gone through the thought _process_ of analyzing the design context and making design decisions
of which the report is only a documentation.

Acceptable levels of AI use for this assignment are (based on the [AI Assessment Scale (AIAS)](https://aiassessmentscale.com/)):

1. **No AI:**
   Students do not use AI at any point during the assessment.
2. **AI Planning:**
   Students may use AI for planning, idea development, and research.\
   _Condition:_ The final submission needs to show how you have developed and refined these ideas.
3. **AI Collaboration:**
   Students may use AI to assist with specific tasks such as drafting text,
   refining and evaluating their work.\
   _Condition:_ They must critically evaluate and modify any AI-generated content they use.

The following is not allowed for this assignment:

  * Using AI beyond this scope.
  * Using AI on Level 2 or Level 3 without fulfilling the conditions.
  * Using AI to a greater extent than is stated in the submission (we assume Level 1 "No AI" if there is no statement).

Make use of the offered Q&A slots with the examiner if you are unsure how to use AI and/or report on it.

**Possible Consequences:**

  * We may call for an oral follow-up to discern whether each group member actually attained the learning goal.
    Negative outcomes result in a failing grade and a report to BTH's disciplinary committee.
